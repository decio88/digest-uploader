Privacy policy

Digest Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: only files it creates in the owner's Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other files.
  • What it collects: nothing. No personal data, usage data or Google user data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Where data goes: the only data transferred is the owner's own digest file, uploaded to the owner's own Drive.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 29 September 2026.
